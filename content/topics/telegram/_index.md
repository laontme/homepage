Telegram has been part of my work in several forms: the entry point to a product, the interface for community tasks, and the place where a team receives operational updates.

At GVWS, I owned the backend connecting bot invitations, Mini App login, campaign routing, and separate account-level and campaign-level referrals. At Open Builders, my contributions included Telegram subscription checks, bot commands, and investor notifications. At Viceseason, I connected store order and payment flows to Telegram notifications. AGNT GM took a different direction: a builder for creating and deploying Telegram bots, with reusable building skills for coding agents.
