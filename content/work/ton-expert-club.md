At TON Expert Club, I co-founded and led the technical side of GVWS. I owned backend architecture, integrations, task verification, deployment, and most operational work; a frontend developer owned the interface.

The work was not a generic “campaign platform” abstraction. It was a real product backend with configurable task mechanics, external state checks, rewards, referrals, ambassador flows, winner selection, and TON/token distributions.
