At TON Expert Club, I co-founded GVWS and led its technical work. I owned the backend architecture, integrations, task verification, deployment, and most operational work. A frontend developer owned the interface.

GVWS connected partner campaigns to actions in external applications, Telegram, and TON wallets. I built the rules and state around those actions: task completion, rewards, referrals, final eligibility checks, winner selection, and prize distribution.

The role combined building the product with running its backend in a small team. It required following an integration through to the local records and operational processes that depended on it.
