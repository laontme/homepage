The Viceseason store was a Laravel commerce product for an independent fashion label. I worked across the operational parts of the purchase path: payment verification, carrier pricing and order creation, product availability, multilingual checkout, customer emails, Telegram notifications, and deployment.

This is not presented as a novel commerce engine. It is evidence that I can take responsibility for the unglamorous pieces that make a store actually sell, ship, and communicate.
