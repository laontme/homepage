Viceseason’s online store connected an international storefront to the work of taking payment, preparing an order, arranging delivery, and keeping people informed.

I worked across that purchase-to-fulfilment path in Laravel: payment verification and result pages, product availability, multilingual checkout, carrier pricing and order creation, customer emails, Telegram notifications, and deployment.

Customer-facing and internal interfaces used the same underlying purchase data. Checkout collected the order; payment providers reported its financial state; shipping integrations supplied delivery details; messages brought the relevant information to customers and the team.

The work evolved as the store added payment providers, delivery options, countries, and catalogue changes. My contributions connected those additions to the existing application and its operations.
