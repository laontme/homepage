I worked within the Notcoin team on integrations and operational tooling around its Telegram-native game.

My contributions connected external services to product state and prepared claim records for token distribution. Destination-specific exports handled the identifiers, addresses, memos, and file layouts expected by different recipients. Eligibility work preserved the distinction between a rejected wallet and a provider that could not return an answer.

I also added internal Telegram commands for changing challenge bonus state and inspecting community chat administrators. The bot served as a focused management interface alongside the customer-facing Mini App.
